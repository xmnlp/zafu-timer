Zafu Timer

Support

Something not working, or a question about Zafu Timer? Write to support@vinkapp.se. Every message is read by a person.

The bell did not sound

Check that BELL is chosen under the disc, and that the volume is up. The bell follows the media volume, which you change with the side buttons while the app is open. The Ring/Silent switch does not silence the bell, but a volume turned all the way down does. The app shows a note under the disc when that is the case.

The countdown is not on the Lock Screen

The countdown is a Live Activity. Open Settings on your device, find Zafu Timer in the list of apps, and switch on Live Activities.

Changing the length

Press minus or plus beside the number. Hold either one to move quickly. A sit can be from 1 to 60 minutes.

Starting and stopping

Press BEGIN at the bottom of the disc. Fifteen seconds of settling come first, and then the sit begins. Stop ends a sit at any time.

Sitting without any sound

Choose SILENT under the disc. Nothing sounds and nothing vibrates. When the sit ends, the disc turns dark and shows the minutes you sat.

What the app runs on

iPhone and iPad with iOS 18.2 or iPadOS 18.2, or later.
