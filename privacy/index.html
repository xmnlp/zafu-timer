Zafu Timer

Privacy policy

Zafu Timer collects no data about you. Nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

What the app keeps on your device

The app remembers three things so that it works the way you left it:

  • the length you chose,
  • whether you chose the bell or silence,
  • while a sit is running, which part of the sit it is in and when it ends.

This is stored on your device only. It is not sent to me or to anyone else, and it is removed when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • It has no account and asks for no name, email address or other personal details.
  • It makes no network connections.
  • It contains no analytics, no advertising and no code from other companies.
  • It does not use the microphone, the camera, your location or your contacts.

The Lock Screen countdown

While you sit, the countdown is shown on the Lock Screen as a Live Activity. Your device draws it by itself. Nothing about it is sent anywhere.

What Apple handles

You get the app from the App Store, which Apple runs under its own privacy policy. If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may show me summary figures such as the number of downloads and crashes. Those figures do not identify you.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, the new version is published on this page.

Contact
